Small Business Scheduling Assistant proof of concept · Effective September 27, 2026

The Small Business Scheduling Assistant proof of concept offers text messages about appointment scheduling. Messages may include responses to requests, available times, pending request status, appointment confirmations, changes, cancellations, and support replies. A requested time is not confirmed until we tell you that the appointment is confirmed.

Enrollment and consent

You may receive program texts only after you have opted in to receive scheduling messages from the Small Business Scheduling Assistant proof of concept. Before enrollment, the person enrolling you must explain the text program and ask for your consent. Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of purchasing services. We will use your consent only for this program and will honor opt-out requests.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your scheduling activity and replies. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's rates and terms also apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. After your opt-out is processed, you may receive one confirmation text, and then no further program texts unless you opt in again.

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at ealeman.kikito@gmail.com.

For appointment changes or cancellations, contact us directly; texting STOP only stops program texts.

Delivery and service

Message delivery is subject to network availability and may be delayed or unsuccessful. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on text messages for emergencies. We may change or discontinue this text program, subject to applicable law.

Privacy and contact

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle information for this program. Questions about these terms or the program can be sent to ealeman.kikito@gmail.com.
