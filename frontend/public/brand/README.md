# Smart Scheduling Assistant logo

`mark.svg` is the editable master artwork. It is a black-and-white, nonhuman face with bookworm glasses, freckles, and a small antenna. The root-level `favicon.svg` uses heavier lines for 16–32 px displays. Both have a transparent outer background and a white face, so the expression stays legible on light or dark surfaces.

Use `mark.svg` at 32 px or larger, with equal width and height. Use `favicon.svg` for browser icons and anywhere below 32 px. `favicon.ico` supplies 16, 32, and 48 px raster sizes for browsers that need ICO. Keep the artwork square and provide accessible text such as “Smart Scheduling Assistant” when it conveys the app identity. The SVG files are the source of truth; regenerate raster variants after editing them.

Asset paths from the owner app root: `/brand/mark.svg`, `/favicon.svg`, and `/favicon.ico`.
